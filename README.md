## 📖 Project Overview

This project delivers a **complete customer segmentation pipeline** for a multi-year retail dataset spanning **18,484 customers** and **$29.36M in revenue**.

The goal was to move beyond raw transactional reporting and answer the questions that actually drive retention strategy:

- **Who are our best customers?** (VIP identification)
- **Where is revenue concentrated?** (Pareto analysis)
- **Which customers are at risk?** (Churn detection)
- **How much revenue is on the line?** (Dollar-value risk)
- **What actions should we take?** (Segment-specific plays)

The final solution combines **advanced SQL** (window functions, CTEs) with a **Power BI semantic model** to deliver a two-page executive dashboard.
